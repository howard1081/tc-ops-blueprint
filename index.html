Design Principles (agreed to date)

  • Many small workers, one shared database. Specialized bots are easier to diagnose and fix; the Airtable base is the only way they communicate.
  • Event-driven, not calendar-driven. TN deadlines move (RF654 delivery restarts the clock); phases are entered by events, with the original day buckets kept as the human mental model.
  • No worker moves without a gate. Machine gates (status fields) govern routine handoffs; judgment, money-adjacent, and outbound-to-other-side actions are vetted by the Manager worker's human-authored rulebook — rule misses escalate to the human by Twilio SMS. Wire verification and rulebook sign-off stay human forever.
  • Rules as data, not guesswork. Rolodexes, TAR form catalog, deadline rules, SOPs per worker, and templates all live in the database — workers retrieve, never improvise.
  • Everything audited. Every action, gate pass, and email logs to an append-only audit trail — satisfies TN REC 3-year retention / 24-hour retrieval and makes failure diagnosis fast.
  • Branded, consistent output. All outbound communication uses approved templates with the brand kit: logo, color palette, disclaimers.
  • Humans can run it manually. The database is designed so the process works with zero bots — the automation is added worker by worker on top of a working manual system.
