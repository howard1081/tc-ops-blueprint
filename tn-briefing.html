Tennessee / TAR Deep-Dive

TC specifics for East Tennessee: the regulatory frame, the TAR form vocabulary, RF401 deadline mechanics, and the regional closing conventions the system must branch on.

1 · The Regulatory Frame

  • Regulator: Tennessee Real Estate Commission (TN REC, Dept. of Commerce & Insurance) — licenses brokers/affiliate brokers and audits broker files for recordkeeping, disclosure, and trust-account compliance.
  • Record retention (hard system requirement): T.C.A. § 62-13-312(b)(6) — all transaction records preserved 3 years after consummation. Electronic records allowed (Rule 1260-02-.40) but must be retrievable in organized form within 24 hours of a Commission inspection request, with a written retention schedule. The archive workflow must produce an indexed per-file archive that passes the 24-hour retrieval test.
  • Escrow: T.C.A. § 62-13-321 — broker escrow records (depositor, deposit date, withdrawal date, payee) kept 3 years. Earnest money handled per contract terms; broker responsible at all times.
  • Licensing boundary: the operation is licensed, but automated comms should still avoid anything that reads as negotiation or advice — templates stay factual/status-only, and judgment calls route to the agent.

2 · The TAR Form System (the document vocabulary)

Tennessee REALTORS® publishes the statewide residential form library; re-issued each January with occasional mid-year changes (e.g., the July 19, 2024 post-NAR-settlement compensation rewrite). The system keys on RF numbers — stable identifiers for document classification.

Local boards stack their own addenda on RF401. East TN boards: East Tennessee REALTORS (Knoxville), NETAR (Tri-Cities), Great Smoky Mountains AOR (Sevier Co.), River Counties AOR, Greater Chattanooga. Intake must capture which board's addenda apply — a wrong-county addendum creates enforceability questions at close.

3 · RF401 Deadline Mechanics (the Deadline Engine rule set)

All periods run from the Effective Date = Binding Agreement Date (last signature + communicated acceptance).

The Inspection → Resolution two-stage clock (TN's trickiest deadline):
  1. The Inspection Period runs its stated days.
  2. Buyer's delivery of RF654 ends the inspection period early and starts the Resolution Period — the delivery day counts, even before the stated deadline.
  3. By the Resolution Deadline one of three must exist or the contract auto-terminates with EM back to buyer: (a) executed RF655, (b) buyer's written as-is acceptance (RF656), or (c) a written extension amendment.
  4. Performance dates on a weekend/federal holiday roll to the next business day — the engine needs a TN holiday calendar and actual delivery-date tracking, not just contract day counts.
  5. Post-repair: RF656 also carries the buyer's termination right if seller misses the repair-completion deadline.

4 · East Tennessee Closing Convention (the big regional variable)

Tennessee has no attorney-closing statute, but practice splits by county:

Closing modelWhere
Title-company closingsKnoxville metro core (Knox, Blount, Anderson), Chattanooga (Hamilton), Nashville, Memphis
Attorney-led closingsMuch of rural East TN by custom — Greene, Hawkins, Cocke, Sevier, Claiborne, Monroe, Polk and surrounding counties

System implication: the deal record's Closing Model is set at intake from the property's county (lookup table, overridable). It changes the closing-prep branch: who gets the commission demand, who issues the settlement statement, who confirms recording, and the final-week templates. The rolodex carries both title-closer and closing-attorney contact types.

5 · Other TN-Specific Items

  • Property condition disclosure (T.C.A. § 66-5-201 et seq.): required on most residential sales unless exempt (new construction, foreclosure → RF203 path). Tracker fields: required/exempt, delivered date, buyer-acknowledged date.
  • Earnest money: holder can be a title company OR broker trust account (differs by region/office — captured at intake). Receipt confirmation is a first-week checklist item with escalation.
  • Post-NAR settlement (July 2024 forms): buyer-side compensation lives in RF141/RF142 (+ any RF702 agreement); every buyer file must show the executed buyer-rep agreement, and compensation documentation must match the settlement statement at close.
  • East TN market color: Sevier County (Gatlinburg/Pigeon Forge) = investor cabin/STR deals — often cash, 14–21 day timelines, attorney closings, HOA/resort assessments, STR-permit due diligence. The cash-deal branch (no financing/appraisal contingencies) gets real use here.
  • Market precedent: SmoothTC is an existing TN-focused TC automation service — validation that this model sells in this market, and a feature-parity benchmark.
